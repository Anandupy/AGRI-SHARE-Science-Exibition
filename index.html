📖 About the AGRI-SHARE Project

School Science & Technology Exhibition Project — Digital Public Infrastructure for Rural Mechanization.

1. The Problem: Costly Machinery

In India, over 86% of operational farm holdings belong to small and marginal farmers (<2 hectares). Modern farm mechanization (tractors, rotavators, harvesters) increases crop yields and reduces human drudgery, but purchasing a single tractor or harvester requires ₹5,00,000 to ₹25,00,000 in capital.

Smallholders typically utilize this equipment for only 20 to 40 hours per year. Purchasing leads to heavy rural indebtedness, high bank loan interest, and idle machinery rusting in village yards for 10–11 months annually.

2. How Shared Equipment Helps

AGRI-SHARE introduces a localized sharing platform connecting machine owners with nearby farmers. Instead of capital ownership, farmers use machinery on a flexible pay-per-use basis.

This converts an unbearable fixed capital liability into small, affordable operational expenses. Smallholders save up to 80% on mechanization, while equipment owners generate supplemental annual income.

3. Primary Backend: Google Forms & Sheets

The system requires zero paid databases or subscriptions:

  • Google Forms: Collects equipment listings and search requests.
  • Google Sheets: Functions as the authoritative source of truth for machine records, rates, and availability.
  • Google Apps Script: Provides the REST API for live querying and matching without commercial cloud fees.

4. Prototype Limitations

  • Simulated Data: When Google Apps Script is not configured, records and distances (e.g. 2 km) are simulated for demonstration.
  • Distance Matching: Live GPS radius requires internet telematics hardware on machines.
  • Phone Privacy: Direct contact numbers are masked on public cards to protect farmer privacy.
⚠️ Mandatory Exhibition Disclaimer: “This is an educational prototype. Sample listings and some distance figures are simulated. Seasonal recommendations are rule-based and should be verified with local agricultural experts. Live equipment availability depends on the configured Google Sheets integration.”

Technical Implementation Architecture

Clean separation between Frontend, Google Data Collection, and Google Sheets Source of Truth.

1. Frontend UI

Vanilla HTML5, CSS3, JavaScript. 7 core pages, interactive calculator, and dynamic seasonal calendar.

2. Google Forms

"List Your Agricultural Equipment" and "Find Agricultural Equipment" collecting farmer inputs.

3. Google Sheets Master

"AGRISHARE EQUIPMENT DATABASE" storing ID, Equipment, Owner, Village, Rate, Status, Best Month, Date, Contact.

4. Apps Script Engine

Executes live matching queries (doGet / doPost) and returns filtered JSON results to the website.
