📖 Ma'am's 6-Step Implementation Summary

STEP 1 — Create Database: Create Google Sheet named AGRISHARE EQUIPMENT DATABASE with columns: ID, Equipment, Owner, Village, Rate/hr, Status.

STEP 2 — Create Form 1: Create Google Form "List Your Agricultural Equipment" for owners.

STEP 3 — Create Form 2: Create Google Form "Find Agricultural Equipment" with Village, Equipment required, Required date, Number of hours.

STEP 4 — Create Google Site: Name it AGRISHARE. Banner: AGRI-SHARE: A Smart Agricultural Equipment Sharing Platform. Add 3 large buttons: 🚜 FIND EQUIPMENT, ➕ LIST EQUIPMENT, 💰 CALCULATE SAVINGS.

STEP 5 — Insert Forms: On the Google Site, click Insert → Forms → Select "Find Agricultural Equipment".

STEP 6 — Results Page: Add table with 🟢/🔴 status dots and the ⭐ Recommended Option card.

STEP 10 — Savings Calculator: ₹5,00,000 price, 30 hours, ₹800/hr → ₹24,000 hiring vs ₹1,20,000 owning.
